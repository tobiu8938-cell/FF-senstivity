    FF Sens Lab · Engine

    How the generator works

    No guesswork — every value is calculated from your actual hardware tier and playstyle.

    1

    Device Detection

    You pick your exact model (or search it) and we pull its known hardware profile — chipset class, RAM tier, and whether it's a gaming-tuned device.

    2

    Performance Tiering

    Your device is scored into Entry, Mid, High, or Flagship tier. Higher-tier hardware unlocks faster base sensitivity and a wider safe DPI range.

    3

    Playstyle Weighting

    Rush, Sniper, or Balanced multipliers reshape the tier's base values — boosting close-range and Free Look for rushers, or scope precision for snipers.

    4

    Final Settings Output

    You get a ready-to-use loadout — General, Red Dot, 2x, 4x, Sniper, and Free Look sensitivity plus DPI, fire button, and graphics recommendations.
